What works in this prototype?

This prototype is designed for iPhone and handles only calls opened through a Kovo invite link. It cannot listen to Phone, WhatsApp, FaceTime or Google Meet. Microphone access requires HTTPS (or localhost).

Without a configured TURN relay, some networks cannot connect. This prototype does not include a paid relay. No raw audio is saved. Text stays in browser memory and travels to your peer over the encrypted WebRTC data channel. Signaling is temporarily held on this server.

On iPhone: open the HTTPS site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Installing does not grant access to other calls.

Experimental local speech transcription

Your browser may send microphone audio to its speech-recognition provider. This feature only transcribes your microphone, not the remote audio track. Each caller must enable it separately. Browser and language support varies. No provider or accuracy guarantee.

No automatic messages. No hidden recording. No transcript storage on the server.
